Family Token

Android app for parents

Turn “How long can I play?” into a clear, fair agreement. Every child gets their own token budget per day, week or month, and you redeem a token with one tap.

Own balance per child

One rule, separate tokens. Presets for media, sweets, gaming and tablet.

Time-based tokens

“1 token = 30 minutes” with a live countdown and a nudge when time is up.

Widget

See balances and redeem straight from your home screen.

Both parents, kid tablet

Join a second phone via QR code. Kids see their coins, view only.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking or analytics SDKs, no selling of data. Use it fully offline, or sync via an anonymous or Google account. Delete your account and all cloud data inside the app. See the privacy policy.
